The map below is a sandboxed iframe. It reports its own height to this page over postMessage, and this page resizes it — the same protocol every incumbent ships, reimplemented here rather than taking a dependency on an unmaintained library.

Resize this window — the frame reports a new height and the embed follows. The host validates the message on frame identity, not just origin, so another frame on this page cannot drive the resize.
