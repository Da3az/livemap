Demo article

The map below is the published map in a sandboxed iframe, the way a publisher pastes it from the Embed tab. It polls a small pointer file and redraws when the newsroom publishes again; publish a change in the editor and watch it arrive here within seconds.

embed-host.js on this page grows the frame when the map's title, key and credits need more room than the frame gives them. It checks every resize message against the frame that sent it, its shape and a height ceiling, so nothing else on the page can drive it.
